### 2. **What is Reassignment?**
Time-frequency reassignment is a post-processing technique applied to spectrograms (typically STFT-based) to sharpen the energy concentration in time-frequency space by relocating spectral energy toward its “center of gravity” in time and/or frequency. It is **not** a standalone transform, but an enhancement to time-frequency representations.

It is commonly used in:
- High-resolution spectrogram generation
- Synchrosqueezing (closely related)
- Improving readability/accuracy of time-frequency analysis
