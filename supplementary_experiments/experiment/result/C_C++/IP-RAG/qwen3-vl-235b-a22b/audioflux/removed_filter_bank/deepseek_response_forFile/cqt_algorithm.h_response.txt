### 2. **What is CQT?**
- The Constant-Q Transform is a time-frequency analysis method where frequency bins are spaced logarithmically (octave-based), mimicking human auditory perception.
- It is *not* a standard STFT; it's a specialized transform optimized for music/audio with harmonic structure.
- CQT is foundational for many MIR tasks: chroma, pitch estimation, tuning detection, etc.
