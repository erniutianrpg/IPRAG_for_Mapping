### 2. **What is WPT?**
- WPT is a **time-frequency analysis tool**, used for decomposing signals into basis functions at multiple scales and frequencies.
- It is a **foundational signal processing transform**, often used in:
  - Denoising,
  - Feature extraction (e.g., for classification),
  - Compression,
  - Spectral analysis in non-stationary signals.

It is **not** itself a feature extractor (like MFCC or spectral centroid), nor is it specifically music-oriented (e.g., pitch, onset). It is a **transform**, similar in nature to STFT, DWT, or synchrosqueezing — i.e., a *core time-frequency decomposition algorithm*.
