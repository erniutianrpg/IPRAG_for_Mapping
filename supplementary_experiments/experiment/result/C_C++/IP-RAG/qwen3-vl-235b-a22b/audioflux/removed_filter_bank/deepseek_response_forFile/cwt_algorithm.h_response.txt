### 2. **What is CWT?**
- CWT is a **time-frequency analysis** technique, analogous to STFT but with variable resolution (high time resolution at high frequencies, high frequency resolution at low frequencies).
- It produces a complex-valued time-scale representation (often converted to time-frequency via scale-to-frequency mapping).
- Used in signal processing for analyzing non-stationary signals, e.g., audio transients, onset detection, or as a front-end for feature extraction.
