### 2. **What is NSGT?**
- NSGT (Non-Stationary Gabor Transform) is a **time-frequency analysis tool**, a generalization of the Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) and Gabor frames, allowing for adaptive, non-uniform frequency resolution (e.g., logarithmic/constant-Q).
- It is used to build **filter banks** for time-frequency representations — often as a preprocessing step for spectrogram-like representations or for invertible transforms.
- Crucially, NSGT is a **transform algorithm**, not a feature extractor per se — it generates the basis functions (filters) needed for signal decomposition.
