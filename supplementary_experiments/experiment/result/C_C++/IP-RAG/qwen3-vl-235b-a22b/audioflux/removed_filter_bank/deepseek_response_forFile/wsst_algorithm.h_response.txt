### 2. **What is WSST?**
Wavelet Synchrosqueezing Transform is an advanced time-frequency analysis method that improves upon the Continuous Wavelet Transform (CWT) by “reassigning” energy in the time-frequency plane to sharpen ridges (e.g., for better instantaneous frequency estimation). It is closely related to:
- Synchrosqueezing (a reassignment-type technique),
- Time-frequency representations (like spectrograms but with wavelet basis),
- Used in signal analysis for high-resolution TF localization — common in audio, vibration, biomedical signal processing.

In AudioFlux context, this is **not** a basic DSP primitive (like FFT, FIR filter), nor is it feature extraction per se (e.g., MFCC, spectral centroid), nor MIR-specific (e.g., pitch, onset). Instead, it's a **higher-level time-frequency transform**, similar to STFT, CQT, or synchrosqueezed STFT — i.e., part of the *core time-frequency transform layer*.
