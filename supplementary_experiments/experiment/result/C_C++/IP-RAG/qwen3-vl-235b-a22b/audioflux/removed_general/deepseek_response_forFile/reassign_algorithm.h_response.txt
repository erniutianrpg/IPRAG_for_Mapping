### 2. **What is “Reassignment”?**
Time–frequency reassignment is a post-processing technique applied to spectrograms (typically from STFT) to sharpen time-frequency representations by relocating energy to its estimated center of gravity in the time–frequency plane. It is a *refinement* of standard STFT-based spectral analysis — not a standalone transform, but an enhancement built on top of STFT.

This is a well-known technique in digital signal processing literature (e.g., Auger & Flandrin, 1995), often used to improve resolution of spectrograms for audio analysis.
