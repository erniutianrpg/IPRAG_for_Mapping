### 2. **What is SWT?**
- The Stationary Wavelet Transform is a *redundant*, translation-invariant variant of the Discrete Wavelet Transform (DWT).
- It decomposes a signal into multiple scales (approximation and detail coefficients) without downsampling — useful for denoising, feature extraction, and time-frequency analysis.
- It is a **foundational transform**, not a high-level feature extractor or music-specific algorithm.
