### 2. **What is CWT?**
- Continuous Wavelet Transform is a time-frequency analysis tool, analogous to STFT but with variable resolution (high time resolution at high freq, high freq resolution at low freq).
- It is implemented via a bank of bandpass filters (wavelets at different scales/frequencies), often designed analytically (e.g., Morlet, Morse) or via spectral sampling.
- In audio/MIR contexts, CWT is used for:
  - High-resolution time-frequency analysis
  - Feature extraction (e.g., for onset detection, pitch tracking, or auditory modeling)
  - As a front-end for perceptual models or deep learning features

But crucially: **CWT itself is a signal transform**, not a feature extractor per se — it produces a time-scale representation (like a spectrogram, but with log-spaced scales).
