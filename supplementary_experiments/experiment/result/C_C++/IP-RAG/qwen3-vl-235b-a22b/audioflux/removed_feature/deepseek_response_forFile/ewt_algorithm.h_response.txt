### 1. **File Name and Context**
- Filename: `ewt_algorithm.h`
- EWT stands for **Empirical Wavelet Transform**, a time-frequency analysis method that adaptively constructs wavelet filters based on the Fourier spectrum of the signal (introduced by Gilles, 2013).
- This is a *time-frequency transform*, similar in purpose to STFT, CQT, or synchrosqueezing — but with adaptive filter banks derived from signal content.
