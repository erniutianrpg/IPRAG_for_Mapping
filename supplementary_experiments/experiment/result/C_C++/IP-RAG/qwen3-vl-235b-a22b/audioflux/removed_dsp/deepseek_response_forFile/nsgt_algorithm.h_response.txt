### 2. **What is NSGT?**
NSGT stands for **Nonstationary Gabor Transform**, a time-frequency representation method that generalizes the Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) using non-uniform, adaptive time-frequency tiling — often used for perceptually relevant or signal-adaptive analysis. It is a *time-frequency transform*, closely related to wavelets and Gabor frames, but with flexible (non-stationary) window placement.

Key characteristics:
- It is a **transform**, not a feature extractor per se.
