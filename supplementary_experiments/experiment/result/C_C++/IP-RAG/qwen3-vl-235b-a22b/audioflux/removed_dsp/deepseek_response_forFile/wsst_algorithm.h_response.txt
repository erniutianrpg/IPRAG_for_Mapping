### 2. **What is WSST?**
Wavelet Synchrosqueezing Transform is an advanced time-frequency analysis technique that improves upon continuous wavelet transform (CWT) by reassigning energy in the time-frequency plane to sharpen ridges (instantaneous frequencies). It is closely related to:
- Continuous wavelet transform (CWT)
- Synchrosqueezing (a class of time-frequency reassignment methods)
- Often used for high-resolution time-frequency analysis, e.g., in audio, vibration, biomedical signal processing.

In AudioFlux context, this is clearly a **time-frequency transform**, not a feature extractor per se (like MFCC), nor a filter bank construction (though it may use filter banks internally), nor MIR-specific (e.g., pitch/onset).
