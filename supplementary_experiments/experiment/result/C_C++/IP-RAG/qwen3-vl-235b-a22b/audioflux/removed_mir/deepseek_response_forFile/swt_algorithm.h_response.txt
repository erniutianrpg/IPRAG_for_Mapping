### 2. **What is SWT?**
- Stationary Wavelet Transform is a *translation-invariant* variant of the Discrete Wavelet Transform (DWT).
- It is a **time-frequency transform**, used for signal decomposition at multiple scales, often in denoising, feature extraction, or multi-resolution analysis.
- Unlike STFT (Short-Time Fourier Transform), it uses wavelets instead of fixed sinusoidal basis functions, but it still belongs to the broader category of *time-frequency analysis*.
