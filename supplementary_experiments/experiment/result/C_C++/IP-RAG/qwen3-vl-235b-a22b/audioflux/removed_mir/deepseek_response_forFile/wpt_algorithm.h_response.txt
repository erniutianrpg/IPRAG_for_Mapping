### 2. **What is WPT?**
WPT = **Wavelet Packet Transform**, a generalization of the Discrete Wavelet Transform (DWT), where both approximation and detail coefficients are further decomposed recursively. It provides a richer time-frequency representation than DWT and is used in signal analysis, feature extraction, denoising, compression, etc.

This is a **time-frequency transform**, specifically a *multiresolution analysis* method based on wavelets.
