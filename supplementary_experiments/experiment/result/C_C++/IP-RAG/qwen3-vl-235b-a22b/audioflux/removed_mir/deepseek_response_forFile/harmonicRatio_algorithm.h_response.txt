### 3. **What is Harmonic Ratio?**
In MIR/audio processing, *harmonic ratio* (or *harmonicity*) is a feature that quantifies how much of the signal energy lies in harmonic partials vs. noise/inharmonic components. It’s typically computed from the spectral domain — e.g., by comparing magnitude of peaks aligned to harmonic series vs. residual or broadband energy.

Thus, it is **not** a raw DSP primitive (like FFT, filtering), but rather a higher-level **feature** derived from spectral analysis.
