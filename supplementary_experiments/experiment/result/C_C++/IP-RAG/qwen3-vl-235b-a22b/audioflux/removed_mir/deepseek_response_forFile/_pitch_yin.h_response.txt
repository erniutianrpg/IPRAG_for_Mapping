### 2. **What is YIN?**
- YIN (De Cheveigné & Kawahara, 2002) is a *pitch tracking* algorithm that operates on the time-domain signal.
- It computes a modified autocorrelation function, identifies local minima (troughs), and selects the most likely period based on a cost function and thresholding.
- It is **not** a spectral transform (like STFT), nor a filter bank or cepstral feature extractor.
- It is a *feature extraction* technique — specifically, extracting **fundamental frequency (F0)** over time, which is a core *audio feature*.
