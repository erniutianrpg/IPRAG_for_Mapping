### 4. **Critical Differentiator: Pre vs Post Process**
- In distributed camera:
  - **Sink side**: Camera captures → *preprocess* (resize, encode, frame rate control) → send over channel.
  - **Source side**: Receive → *postprocess* (decode, convert, adjust) → deliver to app.

- Lives in `data_process/src/pipeline/`
