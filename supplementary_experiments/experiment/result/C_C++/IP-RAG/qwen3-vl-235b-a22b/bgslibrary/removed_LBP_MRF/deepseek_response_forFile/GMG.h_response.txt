### 2. **What is GMG?**
The **GMG** algorithm (Godbehere–Matsukawa–Goldberg) is a **non-parametric**, **per-pixel** background modeling method that uses:
- A **mixture of Gaussians** (but with a *Bayesian* update rule and per-pixel histogram-based learning),
- A **probabilistic foreground decision** based on the number of matching samples,
- And is known for robustness to illumination changes and dynamic backgrounds.

While it uses Gaussian components, it differs from standard GMM (e.g., Stauffer & Grimson) in its update strategy and use of kernel density estimation-like sampling.

Crucially, OpenCV’s `BackgroundSubtractorGMG` implements this exact algorithm.
