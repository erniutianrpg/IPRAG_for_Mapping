This is a **pixel-level background model** based on **moving window statistics**, specifically a **weighted moving variance** (i.e., a variant of simple statistical background modeling with adaptive weighting over recent frames).

It resembles classic **mean/variance-based background subtraction**, but with explicit weighting over multiple frames — not just a single-frame difference (like SigmaDelta), nor a full Gaussian model.
