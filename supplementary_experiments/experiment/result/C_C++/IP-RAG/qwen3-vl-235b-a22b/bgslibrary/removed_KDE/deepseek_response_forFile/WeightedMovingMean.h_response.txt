### 2. **What is “Weighted Moving Mean”?**
- This is a variant of *moving average* background modeling, where past frames are weighted (e.g., exponentially or linearly) to compute a background estimate.
- Classic moving mean:  
  `B_t = α·I_{t−1} + (1−α)·B_{t−1}`  
  Weighted moving mean may use more than one past frame with custom weights (here, two previous frames suggest a 3-frame window: current, prev1, prev2).
- This is a **pixel-level**, **adaptive**, **non-statistical** (i.e., not Gaussian-based) background modeling approach — similar in spirit to SigmaDelta, PBAS, or simple moving average methods.
