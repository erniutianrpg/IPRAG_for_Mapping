### 2. **What is “Weighted Moving Mean”?**
- The name suggests a background modeling approach based on a *moving mean* (i.e., exponential or sliding average) of pixel intensities, with *weights* applied—likely to recent frames more heavily.
- This is a classic **pixel-level background modeling** technique, similar in spirit to:
  - **SigmaDelta**: uses simple per-pixel background estimation and differencing (often a running average).
  - **PBAS**: uses pixel-level adaptive models with thresholds.
  - **TwoPoints**: uses two previous frames for background estimation (very close in idea).
