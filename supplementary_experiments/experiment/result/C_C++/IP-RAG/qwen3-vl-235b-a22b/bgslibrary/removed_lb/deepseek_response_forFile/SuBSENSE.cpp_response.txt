### 3. **Understanding SuBSENSE**
- SuBSENSE (Self-balanced Subspace-based Background Subtraction) is a multi-feature background subtraction algorithm that combines:
  - **LBSP** (Local Binary Similarity Pattern) for texture modeling,
  - Color distance,
  - Sample-based background modeling (like ViBe),
  - And adaptive thresholds.
- It was introduced in the paper *"SuBSENSE: A Universal Change Detection Method with Local Adaptive Sensitivity"* (2015), and its implementation in BGSLibrary is built on top of the `lbsp` module.
