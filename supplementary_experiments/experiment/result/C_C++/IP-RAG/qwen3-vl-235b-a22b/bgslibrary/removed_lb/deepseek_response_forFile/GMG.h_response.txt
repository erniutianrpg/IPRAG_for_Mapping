### 2. **What is GMG?**
- GMG = **Gaussian Mixture Model** background subtraction.
- It models each pixel as a mixture of K Gaussian distributions, updating weights and means/variances over time.
- This is a classic parametric background modeling approach, distinct from non-parametric (e.g., KDE, ViBe, VuMeter) or texture/MRF-based methods.
