### 1. **File Context & Purpose**
- The class is named `WeightedMovingVariance`.
- It inherits from `IBGS` (Interface for Background Subtraction), indicating it's a background subtraction algorithm.
- The core logic computes a **weighted moving average** of the last three frames (`img_input`, `img_input_prev_1`, `img_input_prev_2`) to estimate the background mean, then computes a **weighted variance** (using `computeWeightedVariance`) across those frames, and uses the square root of the total variance as a foreground likelihood measure.
- Thresholding is applied to produce binary foreground mask.
- It maintains history of two previous frames — a classic *temporal filtering* approach.
- No use of GMM, KDE, MRF, LBP, fuzzy logic, or multi-layer modeling.

### 2. **Key Algorithmic Characteristics**
- Uses **moving window (3-frame)** with **explicit weights** (e.g., 0.5, 0.3, 0.2).
- Computes **variance** (not just difference) — i.e., measures deviation from a weighted mean.
- This is a **non-parametric**, **pixel-wise**, **adaptive** method based on temporal statistics.
- It resembles classical **moving variance** or **exponentially weighted moving average (EWMA)**-style background modeling, but with fixed weights over 3 frames.
