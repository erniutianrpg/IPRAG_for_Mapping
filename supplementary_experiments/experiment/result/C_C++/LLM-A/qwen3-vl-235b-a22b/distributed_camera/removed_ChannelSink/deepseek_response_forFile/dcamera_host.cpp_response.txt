Crucially:
- It does **not** handle:
  - Channel establishment (SoftBus/session) → that’s `ChannelSource`/`ChannelCommon`.
  - Data encoding/decoding/pre/post-processing → not in `CameraDataPreProcess`, `PostProcess`, or `Common`.
  - HDF/HDI-level virtual device implementation → not `VirtualCameraHdf`.
  - Sink-side logic (receiving commands, local camera control) → not `DistributedCameraSinkMgr`.

Instead, it provides:
- Camera device enumeration & discovery.
- Camera open/close lifecycle management.
- Callback registration.
- Mapping between distributed hardware entities and camera IDs/devices.
- Central coordination point for distributed camera access.
