### Critical Insight:
In OHOS architecture, **proxy classes like `XxxProxy`** (inheriting `IRemoteProxy<T>`) are typically placed under the **module that consumes the service**, i.e., the client side. Since this is `DistributedCameraSourceProxy`, and it proxies `IDistributedCameraSource`, it belongs to the **source-side module that manages the source functionality** — which is precisely what **DistributedCameraSourceMgr** does.

Moreover, the methods (`InitSource`, `RegisterDistributedHardware`, etc.) match exactly the responsibilities described for `DistributedCameraSourceMgr`:
> *"enabling the controller to invoke distributed camera capabilities and use the controlled device's camera for preview, photo capture, video recording..."*

The proxy is the **IPC client stub** used internally by `DistributedCameraSourceMgr` to talk to the sink-side service. In modular organization, such proxy headers are usually co-located with the manager that uses them (or in a subdirectory like `ipc/` under that module), and are considered part of that module’s implementation.
