Now we must choose between **CameraServiceCommon** and **utils**, both plausible.

Key distinction:
- `CameraServiceCommon` emphasizes *shared service capabilities*: protocols, events, metadata settings, controller interfaces — i.e., **domain-specific shared semantics** for the distributed camera service.
- `utils` is more generic: utilities, buffers, indexes, *general* support — often lower-level helpers.

Looking at the content:
- Enums like `DCameraEventResult`, `AuthorizationState`, `AccessControlType`, `DCameraEventType` are **service-level protocol semantics**, not generic utilities.
- Constants like `DISTRIBUTED_HARDWARE_CAMERA_SOURCE_SA_ID`, `CAMERA_PROTOCOL_VERSION_KEY`, `SNAP_SHOT_SESSION_FLAG`, `CAMERA_METADATA_KEY`, etc., are **part of the distributed camera service contract**, used by both source and sink to interpret messages/events/metadata.
- This is not just “utilities” — it's the **shared vocabulary** of the distributed camera service.
