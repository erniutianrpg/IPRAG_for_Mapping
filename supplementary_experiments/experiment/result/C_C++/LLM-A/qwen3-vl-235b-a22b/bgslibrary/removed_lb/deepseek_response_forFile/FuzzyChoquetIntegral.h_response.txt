In background subtraction, it has been used (e.g., in works like *“Fuzzy Choquet Integral for Background Subtraction”* by A. Elgammal et al. or related literature) to combine multiple cues (e.g., color, texture, motion) using fuzzy measures for more robust foreground detection.
