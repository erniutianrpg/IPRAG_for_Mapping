### 2. **What is GMG?**
- GMG stands for **Gaussian Mixture-based Background/Foreground Segmentation**.
- It is a well-known background subtraction algorithm that models each pixel as a mixture of Gaussians (similar to MOG/MOG2), but with a different learning and update strategy—specifically, it uses per-pixel Bayesian updating and a probabilistic approach based on kernel density estimation over time.
- Though related to GMMs, GMG is distinct from standard GMM (e.g., MOG) and was introduced by Godbehere et al. (2012).
- In OpenCV, `BackgroundSubtractorGMG` is a separate class from `BackgroundSubtractorMOG2`.
