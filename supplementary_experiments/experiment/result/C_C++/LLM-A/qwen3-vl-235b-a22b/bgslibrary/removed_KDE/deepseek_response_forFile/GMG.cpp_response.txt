### 2. **What is GMG?**
- GMG stands for **Gaussian Mixture-based Background/Foreground Segmentation**, introduced by Zivkovic & van der Heide (2006), and later integrated into OpenCV as `BackgroundSubtractorGMG`.
- It models each pixel as a mixture of K Gaussian distributions, updating weights based on Bayesian inference and using a probabilistic decision rule to classify foreground/background.
