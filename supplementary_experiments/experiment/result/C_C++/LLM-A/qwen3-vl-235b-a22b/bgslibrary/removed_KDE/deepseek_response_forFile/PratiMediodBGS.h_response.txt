### 2. **Algorithm Identification**
The class name `PratiMediodBGS` strongly suggests this implements the **Prati et al. background subtraction algorithm**, which is known to use **robust median/medoid estimation** over a history of samples per pixel, with adaptive thresholds and sampling rate.

This algorithm was introduced in:
> Prati, A., Mikic, I., Trivedi, M. M., & Cucchiara, R. (2003). *Detecting moving shadows: Algorithms and evaluation*. IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence, 25(7), 918–923.

It uses:
- A fixed-size history buffer per pixel.
- Median (or medoid) estimation for background.
- Two thresholds (low/high) for foreground detection.
- Sampling rate and weight parameters.

Thus, this is a **pixel-level background modeling algorithm**, robust to noise/outliers, based on **median/medoid**, not Gaussian, SOM, LBP, ViBe, etc.
