### 2. **What is OpenCV’s BackgroundSubtractorKNN?**
- It implements the **K-Nearest Neighbors** background subtraction algorithm.
- It is a *non-parametric*, *pixel-level* method that maintains a history of pixel values and classifies new pixels based on similarity to K nearest neighbors in the history.
- It is **not** the same as the KDE-based non-parametric methods (like VuMeter or KDE module), nor is it Gaussian/GMM-based (like lb or dp).
- It is a well-known standard algorithm, often compared with ViBe, MOG2, etc.
