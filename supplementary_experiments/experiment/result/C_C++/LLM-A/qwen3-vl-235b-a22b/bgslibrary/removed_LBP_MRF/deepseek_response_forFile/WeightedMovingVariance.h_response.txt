### What does “Weighted Moving Variance” suggest?
- It's computing *variance* over time, weighted (likely exponentially or with a sliding window), and using that to detect foreground (e.g., high variance = change = foreground).

- The name resembles classical approaches like *moving average*, *moving variance*, or *exponentially weighted moving average/variance* — common in simple but effective background subtraction.
