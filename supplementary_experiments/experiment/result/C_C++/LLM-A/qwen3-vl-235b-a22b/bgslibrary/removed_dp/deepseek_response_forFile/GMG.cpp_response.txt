### 2. **What is GMG?**
GMG stands for **Gaussian Mixture-based Background Subtraction**, originally proposed by Zivkovic (2004). It models each pixel as a mixture of Gaussians (like GMM), but with a different learning and update strategy—using Bayesian inference and per-pixel histograms of color values, often with a probabilistic decision threshold.

Crucially, **GMG is *not* the same as standard GMM (e.g., MOG/MOG2)**. It is a distinct algorithm, implemented in OpenCV as `BackgroundSubtractorGMG`.
