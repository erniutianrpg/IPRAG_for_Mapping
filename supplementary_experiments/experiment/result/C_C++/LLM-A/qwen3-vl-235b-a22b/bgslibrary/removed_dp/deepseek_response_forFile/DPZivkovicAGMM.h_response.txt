### 2. **What is Zivkovic AGMM?**
- It is a well-known **Gaussian Mixture Model (GMM)**-based background subtraction algorithm, an improvement over Stauffer & Grimson's GMM, proposed by Zivkovic (2004, 2006).
- It uses per-pixel mixture of Gaussians with adaptive learning rate and model selection.
- It is **parametric**, **pixel-level**, and **adaptive**, not non-parametric (like KDE, VuMeter), nor fuzzy (T2F), nor multi-layer (MultiLayer), nor texture-based (LBP_MRF, LBSP).
