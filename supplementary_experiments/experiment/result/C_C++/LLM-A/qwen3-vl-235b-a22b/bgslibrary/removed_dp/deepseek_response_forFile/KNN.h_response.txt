### 2. **What is KNN in background subtraction?**
OpenCV’s `BackgroundSubtractorKNN` implements the **K-Nearest Neighbors** background subtraction algorithm, which:
- Maintains a history of pixel values.
- For each pixel, models background as the K nearest samples in feature space (typically intensity or color).
- Uses distance thresholding and shadow detection.
- Is a well-known, standard algorithm in background subtraction literature.

- LB series (Gaussian/SOM/fuzzy) — different modeling approach;
