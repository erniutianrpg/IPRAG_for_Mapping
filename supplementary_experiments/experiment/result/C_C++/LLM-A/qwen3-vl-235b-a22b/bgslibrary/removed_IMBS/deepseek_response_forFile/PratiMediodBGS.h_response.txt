### 2. **Algorithm Identification**
The algorithm is **Prati-Mediod BGS**, which is a known background subtraction method based on:
- Maintaining a buffer of past pixel values per pixel location.
- Using a *mediod* (not mean or median) as the background model estimate — i.e., the pixel in the buffer that minimizes total distance to all others (L1 or L2).
- Adaptive thresholding with low/high thresholds for foreground detection.
- It is **not** Gaussian-based, not KDE-based, not ViBe, not PBAS, not SigmaDelta, etc.

This method originates from Prati et al. (e.g., “Detecting Moving Objects in Video Sequences Using Robust Background Subtraction” or related works), and is implemented here as part of the `dp` namespace.
