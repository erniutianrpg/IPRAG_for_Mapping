### 2. **What is “Sugeno Integral”?**
The **Sugeno integral** is a type of fuzzy integral used in decision fusion, especially in multi-criteria decision making and fuzzy systems. In background subtraction, it’s often used to fuse multiple cues (e.g., color, texture, motion) using fuzzy measures — commonly seen in **fuzzy-based background modeling**, especially in methods that combine evidence via fuzzy integrals (e.g. Sugeno or Choquet integrals).
