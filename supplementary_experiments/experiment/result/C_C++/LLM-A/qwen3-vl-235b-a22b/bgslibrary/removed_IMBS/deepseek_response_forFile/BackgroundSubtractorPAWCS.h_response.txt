### 2. **What is PAWCS?**
PAWCS (Pixel-level Adaptive Weighted Color Similarity) is a background subtraction algorithm that builds upon **LBSP** (Local Binary Similarity Pattern) features. It uses:
- Local and global word dictionaries (like bag-of-words),
- Adaptive weighting based on spatio-temporal occurrence,
- Color similarity thresholds,
- Moving averages for model adaptation.

It is explicitly mentioned in literature as part of the **LBSP-based family**, alongside SuBSENSE and LOBSTER.
