### 2. **What is HPACK?**
HPACK is the header compression format defined in [RFC 7541](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc7541) for HTTP/2. It is part of the HTTP/2 protocol specification and is used to compress HTTP headers during transmission.
