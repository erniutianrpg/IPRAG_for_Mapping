### 2. **What is HPACK?**
HPACK is the header compression format defined in [RFC 7541](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc7541), used exclusively in **HTTP/2** (and optionally in HTTP/3). It's part of the HTTP protocol layer, not transport or SSL.
