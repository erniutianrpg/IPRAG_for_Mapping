### 2. **Module Definitions Recap**
- **dsp**: *Low-level* DSP — STFT, BFT/STFT-style transforms, spectrograms at different scales, resampling, phase extraction, basic filtering.
- **feature**: *Feature extraction* from spectral/cepstral/time-domain data — Spectral, XXCC (e.g., MFCC), Deconv, Cepstrogram, Temporal, FeatureExtractor.

- **mir**: *Music information retrieval* — pitch estimation, onset detection, HPSS, harmonic analysis, pitch shifting, time stretching — i.e., high-level music semantics.

- The **CQT** is a *time-frequency transform*, but it's **not STFT/BFT** — it's a logarithmic frequency-scale transform, especially common in MIR due to its perceptual relevance (octave-aligned bins).

  - `chroma`: directly used in MIR for tonal analysis.
  - `cqcc`: cepstral features derived from CQT — analogous to MFCC but on CQT; used in music/audio classification.
  - `deconv`: likely for source-filter separation (e.g., vocal tract modeling), relevant to both feature extraction and MIR.
- The interface is low-level (C-style, opaque object, manual memory management), suggesting it's part of a foundational layer — but the *semantic purpose* is strongly aligned with **music processing**, not generic DSP.

- Chroma (strong MIR),
