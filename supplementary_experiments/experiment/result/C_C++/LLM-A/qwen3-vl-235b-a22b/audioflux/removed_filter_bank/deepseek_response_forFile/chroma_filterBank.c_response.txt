### 2. **What is a Chroma Filter Bank?**
- Chroma features represent audio in terms of 12-dimensional pitch class vectors (C, C#, D, ..., B), often derived by projecting spectral energy onto pitch-class-aligned filters.
- Typically built from STFT or CQT spectra.
- Used heavily in music analysis (e.g., in librosa, Essentia, etc.) — squarely in the domain of **Music Information Retrieval (MIR)**.
