### 2. **What is Reassignment?**
- Reassignment is a well-known technique in time-frequency analysis to improve the concentration of energy in spectrograms by relocating spectral components to their "center of gravity" in time and frequency.
- It is typically applied *after* STFT and involves computing group delay and instantaneous frequency to shift bins.
- It is a **time-frequency transform enhancement**, not a feature extractor per se (like MFCC), nor a filter bank or MIR-specific algorithm.
