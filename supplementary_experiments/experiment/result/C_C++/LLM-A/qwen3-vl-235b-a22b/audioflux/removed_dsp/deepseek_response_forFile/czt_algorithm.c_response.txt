### 2. **What is CZT used for?**
- CZT is a **time-frequency transform**, like FFT, but more flexible.
- It's not a *feature extractor* per se (like MFCC, spectral centroid), but rather a *tool* to compute spectra with non-uniform frequency resolution.
- In audio signal processing, CZT can be used for:
  - High-resolution spectral analysis (e.g., for pitch estimation),
  - Zooming into specific frequency bands,
  - Alternative to STFT when non-uniform frequency grids are needed.

But crucially, CZT is a **core transform**, not a higher-level MIR or feature extraction algorithm.
