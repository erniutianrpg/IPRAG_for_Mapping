### 2. **What is Synchrosqueezing?**
Synchrosqueezing is a **time-frequency analysis method** that sharpens the representation of non-stationary signals (e.g., audio) by reassigning energy in the time-frequency plane based on instantaneous frequency estimates. It is closely related to:
- Short-time Fourier transform (STFT)
- Reassignment methods
- Time-frequency concentration improvement

It is **not** a feature extractor per se (like MFCC, spectral centroid), nor a filter bank construction method, nor directly MIR-specific (though MIR may *use* it). Instead, it's a **core time-frequency transform / signal processing algorithm**, part of the foundational signal analysis toolkit.
