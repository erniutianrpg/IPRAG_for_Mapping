### What is EMD?
Empirical Mode Decomposition is a data-driven technique to decompose a signal into intrinsic mode functions (IMFs). It is widely used in time-frequency analysis, especially for non-stationary and nonlinear signals. When combined with the Hilbert transform, it forms the **Hilbert-Huang Transform (HHT)**, used for time-frequency representation.

EMD is **not** a fixed transform like STFT or wavelet; it's adaptive and iterative. It sits at the level of **signal decomposition**, often used as a preprocessing step before feature extraction or spectral analysis.
