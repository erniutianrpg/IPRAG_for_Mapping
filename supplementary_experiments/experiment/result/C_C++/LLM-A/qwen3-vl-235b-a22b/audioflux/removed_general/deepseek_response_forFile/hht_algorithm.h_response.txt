### 2. **What is HHT?**
The **Hilbert–Huang Transform** is a method for analyzing non-stationary and nonlinear signals. It consists of:
- **Empirical Mode Decomposition (EMD)**: decomposes a signal into intrinsic mode functions (IMFs),
- **Hilbert spectral analysis**: computes instantaneous frequency and amplitude from IMFs.

It is *not* a Fourier-based transform (like STFT), nor is it strictly a filter bank or auditory model. It's a time-frequency analysis technique focused on adaptive decomposition.
