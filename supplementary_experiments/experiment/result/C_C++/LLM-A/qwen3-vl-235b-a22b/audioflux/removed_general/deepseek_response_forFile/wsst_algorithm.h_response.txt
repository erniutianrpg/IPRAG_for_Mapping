### 2. **What is WSST?**
- **Synchrosqueezed Wavelet Transform (SST/WSST)** is a time-frequency analysis method that improves time-frequency resolution over standard continuous wavelet transform (CWT) by "reassigning" energy to sharpen ridges.
- It is a *time-frequency representation* technique — more precise than STFT or CWT, used for analyzing non-stationary signals (e.g., audio, vibration).
- Not a feature extractor per se, but a *transform* that yields a high-resolution time-frequency representation.
