### 2. **What is Reassignment?**
In signal processing — especially time-frequency analysis — *reassignment* refers to a technique applied to spectrograms (typically from STFT) to sharpen time-frequency representations by relocating energy to its "center of gravity" in time and/or frequency. This improves resolution beyond the standard STFT grid.

Key points:
- It operates on STFT-like transforms (i.e., after computing a short-time Fourier transform).
- It is a *post-processing* step to refine time-frequency representations.
- It is not a standalone transform, but enhances existing spectral representations.
