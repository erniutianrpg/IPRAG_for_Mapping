### 3. **What is WSST?**
- **Wavelet Synchrosqueezed Transform (WSST)** is a time-frequency analysis technique that improves the resolution of the Continuous Wavelet Transform (CWT) by "reassigning" energy in the time-frequency plane based on phase information.
- It is a **refinement of CWT**, used for high-resolution time-frequency representation — especially useful for non-stationary signals like audio.
- It is **not** a feature extractor per se (like MFCC or spectral centroid), nor is it directly about music semantics (pitch, onset, HPSS), nor just a filter bank construction.
- It is a **low-level signal transform**, akin to STFT, CWT, or reassigned spectrograms — part of the foundational DSP toolkit.
