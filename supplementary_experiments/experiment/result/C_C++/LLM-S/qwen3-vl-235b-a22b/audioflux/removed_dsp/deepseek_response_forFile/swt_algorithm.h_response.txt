### What is SWT?
Stationary Wavelet Transform (SWT) is a *time-frequency transform* that provides a redundant, translation-invariant wavelet decomposition. It is closely related to discrete wavelet transform (DWT) but avoids downsampling, preserving time resolution. It is used for denoising, feature extraction, signal decomposition — often as a preprocessing step for further analysis (e.g., in MIR or feature engineering), but fundamentally it is a **transform**, not a feature extractor per se.
