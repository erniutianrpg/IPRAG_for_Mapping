### What is EMD?
Empirical Mode Decomposition is a data-driven time-frequency analysis method that decomposes a signal into Intrinsic Mode Functions (IMFs). It is often used as a preprocessing step for time-frequency analysis, especially in non-stationary signal contexts (e.g., audio, biomedical signals). When combined with Hilbert transform, it yields the Hilbert-Huang Spectrum.
