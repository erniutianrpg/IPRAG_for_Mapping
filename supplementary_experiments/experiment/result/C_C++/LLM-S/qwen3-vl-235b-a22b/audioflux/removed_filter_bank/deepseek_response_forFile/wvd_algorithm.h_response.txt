### **What is WVD?**
The **Wigner–Ville Distribution (WVD)** is a fundamental *quadratic time-frequency analysis* tool in signal processing. It provides high-resolution joint time-frequency representations but suffers from cross-term interference. It is:
- A **low-level time-frequency transform**, analogous to STFT or Gabor transforms, but non-linear.
- Used in advanced DSP for analyzing non-stationary signals (e.g., transient audio, radar, biomedical signals).
- Not directly a *feature extractor* (like MFCC), nor a *music-specific* algorithm (like pitch estimation), nor a general utility (like I/O or resampling).

It belongs to the foundational **digital signal processing** layer — specifically, time-frequency analysis.
