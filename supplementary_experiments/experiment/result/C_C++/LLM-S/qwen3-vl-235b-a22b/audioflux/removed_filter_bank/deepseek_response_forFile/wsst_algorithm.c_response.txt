### **2. What is WSST?**
- **Wavelet Synchrosqueezing Transform (WSST)** is a time-frequency analysis method that improves the resolution of the Continuous Wavelet Transform (CWT) by “squeezing” energy toward the instantaneous frequency.
- It is a *refinement* of CWT, used for sharper time-frequency representations — especially in audio and vibration analysis.
- Not a feature extractor per se, but a *transform* that produces a high-resolution time-frequency representation.

This places WSST squarely in the domain of **time-frequency transforms**, not raw DSP primitives (like FIR filters), nor high-level feature extraction (like MFCC), nor MIR-specific tasks (like pitch estimation).
