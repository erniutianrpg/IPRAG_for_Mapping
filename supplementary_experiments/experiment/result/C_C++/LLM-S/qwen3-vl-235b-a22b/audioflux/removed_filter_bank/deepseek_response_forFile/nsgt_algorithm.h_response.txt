### **2. What is NSGT?**
The **Nonstationary Gabor Transform (NSGT)** is a *time-frequency transform* that generalizes the Short-Time Fourier Transform (STFT) and Gabor transform by allowing non-uniform, adaptive time-frequency tiling (e.g., logarithmic frequency spacing, variable window lengths). It is used for high-resolution spectral analysis, especially in music/audio processing where perceptual frequency scales (e.g., ERB, mel, octave-based) are desired.

It is **not** a feature extractor per se (like MFCC or spectral centroid), nor is it a high-level MIR task (like pitch estimation). It is a **foundational time-frequency transform**, similar to STFT, but more flexible.
