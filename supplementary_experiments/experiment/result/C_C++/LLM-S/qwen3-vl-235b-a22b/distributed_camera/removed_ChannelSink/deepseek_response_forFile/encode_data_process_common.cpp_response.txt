### **4. Critical Insight: Where does encoding happen?**
In distributed camera architecture:
- **Sink side** (controlled device): captures raw frames → preprocesses → **encodes** → sends over channel.
- **Source side**: receives encoded stream → decodes → post-processes → displays.

- `sourceConfig_`, `targetConfig_`
- `IsConvertible(sourceConfig, targetConfig)` — suggests adapting from one codec/resolution to another
