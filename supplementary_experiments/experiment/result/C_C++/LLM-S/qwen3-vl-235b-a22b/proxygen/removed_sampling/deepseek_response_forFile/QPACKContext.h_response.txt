### 2. **What is QPACK?**
- QPACK is the header compression format used in **HTTP/3** (and also applicable to HTTP/2 in some contexts), standardized in [RFC 9204](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc9204).
- It is a successor to HPACK (used in HTTP/2), designed to handle the head-of-line blocking issues of HPACK over QUIC streams.
- The `QPACKContext` manages the dynamic header table, static table reference, tracking of insertions, blocked inserts, etc.—core logic for *header compression/decompression* in HTTP/3.
