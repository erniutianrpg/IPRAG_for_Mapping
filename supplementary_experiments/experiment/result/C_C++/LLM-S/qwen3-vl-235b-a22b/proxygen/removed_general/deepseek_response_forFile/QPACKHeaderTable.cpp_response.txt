### **2. What is QPACK?**
- QPACK is the header compression format for **HTTP/3**, defined in [RFC 9204](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/rfc9204/).
- It replaces HPACK (used in HTTP/2) and introduces concepts like:
  - Dynamic table with encoder/decoder synchronization,
  - Absolute vs. internal indices,
  - Vulnerability tracking (unacked entries),
  - `minFree` space to avoid blocking due to unacknowledged entries.
- The implementation resides in `proxygen/lib/http/codec/compress/`, which is part of the **HTTP codec layer**, specifically for HTTP/3 support.
