### 2. **What is QPACK?**
- QPACK is the header compression format defined in [RFC 9204](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc9204) for HTTP/3.
- It is the successor to HPACK (used in HTTP/2), designed to avoid head-of-line blocking via separate encoder/decoder streams.
- Implementation lives in `proxygen/lib/http/codec/compress/`, as seen in includes.
