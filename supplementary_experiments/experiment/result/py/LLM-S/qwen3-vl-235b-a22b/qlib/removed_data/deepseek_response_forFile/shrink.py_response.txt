### 2. **What is a Risk Model in Qlib?**
In quantitative finance and Qlib specifically:
- A **risk model** estimates the covariance matrix of asset returns, used for portfolio optimization (e.g., mean-variance), risk decomposition, and factor modeling.
- It is distinct from a **forecast/prediction model** (`qlib.model.base.Model`) which predicts future returns/scores.
- Risk models are typically used *in conjunction* with strategies or portfolio optimizers (e.g., in `strategy` or `contrib` components), but they themselves are infrastructure for risk estimation.
