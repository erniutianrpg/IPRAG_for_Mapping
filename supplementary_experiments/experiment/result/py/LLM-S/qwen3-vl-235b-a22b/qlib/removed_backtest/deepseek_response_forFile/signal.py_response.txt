### 2. **What is a “Signal” in Qlib?**
In Qlib’s architecture:
- A **signal** is typically the output of a model (e.g., predicted alpha score per stock per time), used by strategies to make trading decisions.
- Signals are often time-indexed (`pd.Series`/`pd.DataFrame`) with index = timestamp, columns = instruments.
- The `Signal` interface abstracts how signals are obtained — whether cached, computed on-the-fly, or loaded from file.
- The pattern `ModelSignal(model, dataset)` is exactly how you'd generate predictions over a dataset for backtesting or live use — a core step in **workflow** execution (e.g., in `qrun`, or when running inference).
