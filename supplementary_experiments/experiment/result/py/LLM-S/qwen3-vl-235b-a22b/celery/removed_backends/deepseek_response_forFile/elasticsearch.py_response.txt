### What is a *result backend*?
In Celery, the **result backend** is responsible for storing and retrieving task results (return values, status, traceback). It is distinct from the *broker* (which queues tasks) and is used when `task_ignore_result=False` or `result_backend` is configured.

Result backends are typically implemented as subclasses of `celery.backends.base.BaseBackend` or `KeyValueStoreBackend`. They live in the `celery.backends` namespace — but note: the candidate modules listed are **top-level Celery internal modules**, not the `backends` package itself.
