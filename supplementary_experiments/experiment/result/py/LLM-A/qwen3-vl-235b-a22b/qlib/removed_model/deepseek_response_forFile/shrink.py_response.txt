### 2. **What is a Risk Model?**
In quantitative finance and Qlib specifically:
- A *risk model* estimates the covariance matrix of asset returns, used in portfolio optimization (e.g., mean-variance optimization) to control risk.
- Shrinkage estimators (like Ledoit-Wolf, Oracle Approximating Shrinkage) are standard techniques to improve covariance estimation stability, especially when sample size is small relative to assets.

The class `ShrinkCovEstimator` implements such shrinkage-based risk models — clearly a **risk modeling component**.
