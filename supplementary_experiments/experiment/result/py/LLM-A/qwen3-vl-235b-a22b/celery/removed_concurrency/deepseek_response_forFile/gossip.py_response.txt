### 2. **What is a Bootstep?**
In Celery, `bootsteps` are components of the worker’s startup and runtime lifecycle. They are used to extend or modify worker behavior — e.g., adding consumers, timers, or event handlers. The `Gossip` class is a *ConsumerStep*, meaning it consumes messages (from a dedicated queue) to coordinate with other workers.
