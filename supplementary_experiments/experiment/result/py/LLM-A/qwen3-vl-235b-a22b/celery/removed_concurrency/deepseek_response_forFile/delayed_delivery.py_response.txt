### 2. **What is a Bootstep?**
In Celery, bootsteps are components that plug into the worker’s startup/shutdown lifecycle (via `Consumer` or `Worker`). They are part of the **worker runtime architecture**, used to extend or modify worker behavior (e.g., adding monitoring, custom message handling, transport features).

The `DelayedDelivery` bootstep:
- Runs during worker startup (`start` method).
- Binds queues to delayed delivery exchanges (transport-level).
- Validates configuration relevant to the worker’s execution environment.
- Is tightly coupled with `Consumer`, not with app-level logic, backends, CLI, or loaders.
