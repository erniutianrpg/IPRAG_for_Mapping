### What is a *result backend*?
In Celery, a **result backend** is responsible for storing and retrieving task results (e.g., success/failure states, return values). It is distinct from the *broker* (which handles task queues), and is used when `task_ignore_result=False` or when users explicitly retrieve results via `task.AsyncResult.get()`.

Result backends are typically implemented as subclasses of `celery.backends.base.BaseBackend` or `KeyValueStoreBackend`, and live in the `celery.backends.*` namespace.
