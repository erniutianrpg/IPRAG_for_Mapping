### 3. **Classes Overview**:
- `ResultBase`: Base class for all result types.

- `GroupResult`: Subclass of `ResultSet`, for group tasks; includes `save()`, `restore()`, `as_tuple()`.
- `EagerResult`: For eager (synchronous/local) execution — mimics `AsyncResult` but with immediate values.

These are **abstraction layers over result retrieval and composition**, designed to be backend-agnostic (they delegate to `.backend`), and integrate with the app and vine promises.
