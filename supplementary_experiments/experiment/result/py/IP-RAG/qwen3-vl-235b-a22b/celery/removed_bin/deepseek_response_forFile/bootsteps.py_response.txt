### 2. **What is a “Bootstep”?**
In Celery, **bootsteps** are components that define the startup/shutdown sequence of the worker (or other actors), arranged as a DAG where steps can depend on others. They are used to modularize and compose the worker's initialization and teardown logic (e.g., setting up connections, starting consumers, heartbeat threads, etc.).

This is a core part of how Celery workers are structured internally — not application-level (like task registration), not backend or concurrency pool logic, but rather *how the worker itself boots and shuts down*.
